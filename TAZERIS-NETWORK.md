## Route by need

- Calculator, spreadsheet or seller tool -> Money Factory.
- Ready Telegram utility -> Bot Factory.
- Website fix, small custom Telegram bot or small Android app -> Service Storefront.
- Agent-callable automation API -> App Factory public agent services.

Each destination has its own scope. Do not invent or transfer capabilities, customer claims, prices or guarantees from one factory to another.
