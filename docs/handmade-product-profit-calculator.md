# Handmade product profit planning

Pricing a handmade product becomes clearer when the calculation separates revenue from the costs needed to make and sell one unit. A useful first pass includes materials, direct labor, packaging, seller-paid shipping and marketplace or payment fees.

## Example approach

If materials cost 5, production takes half an hour, your labor rate is 15 per hour, packaging costs 1, and the marketplace/payment fee is 8%, the direct unit cost before the percentage fee is 13.50. At a selling price of 25, the percentage fee is 2.00, leaving 9.50 before overhead, tax and any other omitted business costs.

The important part is not one example number. It is using the same cost logic every time a material price, labor time, shipping assumption or marketplace fee changes. A positive product margin does not automatically mean the whole business is profitable because rent, software, equipment, refunds, advertising and taxes may still matter.
